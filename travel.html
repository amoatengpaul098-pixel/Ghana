TRAVEL SMARTER

Plan your Ghana journey.

Practical reminders for a thoughtful and well-prepared visit.

01

Before you travel

Check current entry requirements with Ghana’s official immigration and consular sources. Keep copies of important documents and confirm accommodation and transport arrangements.

02

Getting around

Intercity buses, shared transport, taxis and app-based rides operate in different areas. Confirm routes and fares before departure; journey times can vary with traffic and road conditions.

03

Weather & seasons

Ghana has a tropical climate, with regional differences. Rainfall patterns vary between the south and north, so check local forecasts and seasonal conditions for your destination.

04

Respect local communities

Ask permission before photographing people, dress appropriately at sacred or formal sites, and follow local guidance at cultural and natural attractions.

05

Responsible tourism

Use local guides where appropriate, respect wildlife and protected areas, reduce litter and support community businesses.

This page is general information, not live travel, safety, immigration or medical advice. Verify current requirements and conditions with official sources before travelling.
